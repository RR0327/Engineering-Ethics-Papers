Easy way to complete a paper in a short time.

---

_Keep it confidential. My personal trick._

**`Read`**:

1. Title
2. Abstract
3. Introduction (first 1–2 paragraphs)
4. Conclusion

**Ask yourself**:

- What problem are they solving?
- Why is it important?
- What did they find?

If you can't answer those three questions, then continue reading those points again.

**`Now read`**:

5. Research Questions
6. Methodology
7. Results
8. Discussion

_Ignore_:

> Long literature review
> Statistics
> Mathematical formulas
> References

Now you should understand how they solved the problem.
