# Google Form Survey

## Research Topic
**Ethics of Collecting User Data in Modern Web Applications**

## Form Description

This survey examines how users understand and respond to data-collection and privacy-consent practices in modern web applications.

Participation is voluntary and anonymous. Do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or other directly identifying information unless required by your institution. The survey should take approximately 5–7 minutes.

# Recommended Google Forms Settings

- Turn off **Collect email addresses**
- Turn off **Limit to 1 response** unless participants must sign in
- Do not request names, phone numbers, exact addresses, or account details
- Make Questions 1–31 required
- Keep Questions 32–34 optional
- Use section-based navigation
- Apply answer-based navigation to Question 1
- Send participants under 18 to the end of the form
- Add a confirmation message after submission
