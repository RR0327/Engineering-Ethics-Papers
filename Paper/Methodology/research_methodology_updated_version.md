# Research Methodology: Ethics of Collecting User Data in Modern Web Applications

A quantitative cross-sectional survey design was used to examine users’ privacy awareness, consent behaviour, experience with data-collection interfaces, and views on ethical regulation.

## Methodology Mapping Matrix

| Methodology Dimension | Research Instrument / Tool | Primary Metrics & Target Data |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Survey Data Collection** | Bilingual Google Form with 26 closed-ended questions; convenience sampling | 154 responses; age group, web-use frequency, privacy concern, cookie and permission behaviour |
| **2. User Awareness and Behaviour Analysis** | Privacy knowledge, consent choices, trust, and perceived dark-pattern questions | Frequencies, percentages, response distributions, and attitude–behaviour patterns |
| **3. Ethical and Regulatory Evaluation** | Questions on consent fairness, legal accountability, and stronger privacy laws | Support for equal reject options, company responsibility, penalties, and government regulation |
| **4. Data Preparation and Anonymization** | Removal of email addresses, duplicate checking, response coding, and data cleaning | An anonymized and analysis-ready survey dataset |
| **5. Statistical Relationship Analysis** | Cross-tabulation, chi-square testing, and Cramér’s V where appropriate | Relationships between privacy concern, awareness, consent behaviour, and trust |
| **6. Reliability and Limitation Assessment** | Questionnaire review, consistency checking, and sample evaluation | Self-report bias, convenience-sampling bias, age-group imbalance, and question-design limitations |
